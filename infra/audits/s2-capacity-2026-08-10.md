The 48 Vault preproduction snapshots on VM 920/921 are the dominant snapshot
count. Thin snapshots share unchanged blocks, so their logical sizes must not be
summed as physical consumption. They still retain changed blocks and increase
metadata and operational risk. Review every snapshot with the Vault owner after
the off-host backup and restore proof. Record an explicit keep/delete decision,
take a current verified backup, and remove only named snapshots with owner
approval. Never bulk-delete from a computed list.

Capacity gates:

- Warning: data 70 percent or metadata 60 percent.
- Critical: data 80 percent or metadata 70 percent.
- Stop new snapshot/stateful deployment work at either critical threshold.

The backup preflight enforces the critical thresholds. Proxmox snapshot cleanup
is not part of the Starfiniti Search deployment authorization.

Do not combine the pending host upgrade with certification deployment. First
complete the off-host backup and restore proof, capture a fresh PVE configuration
backup, announce a maintenance window, review the simulated transaction, upgrade,
reboot if required, and verify every running guest plus storage/network health.
